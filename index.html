01 THE ART

Art doesn't always have to be loud. Sometimes a painting simply holds a feeling.

Each piece at Eliza Art Corner is created slowly and thoughtfully, with an emphasis on atmosphere, emotion and the beauty of everyday moments.

02 ABOUT THE ARTIST

A little about me

Creating paintings with heart.

Welcome to Eliza Art Corner. This is a space where I share my original paintings and the moments, feelings and stories that inspire them.

My work explores beauty in ordinary scenes and the emotions we often associate with places, memories and quiet moments.

Thank you for taking the time to visit my little corner of the art world.
